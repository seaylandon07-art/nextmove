Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one and saves in your browser. A free account syncs your games, drills and progress across your devices and lets you play online.

How do the drills work?

Every game you play is checked by Stockfish in the background. Your mistakes become drills that come back on a schedule (1 day, 3 days, a week…) until you get them right every time.

How do I bring my chess.com or Lichess games?

Open Review, pick Chess.com or Lichess, type your username and tap Load. Pick a game to review, or review them all in the background.

Ratings

What is my play rating?

An Elo rating from your rated games against bots and online players. It moves faster for your first 10 games.

What is game rating?

An estimate of how well you played in one game, based on your accuracy and your opponent, on a scale like chess.com’s.

Why didn’t my rating change?

Online games that end before move 2 are aborted and don’t count. Friend games by code are unrated.

Account

I forgot my password

Tap Sign in → Forgot password, and enter your username and the recovery code you saved when you signed up. If you have neither, email us from the address on your account.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from your account email or include your username, and we’ll delete everything within 7 days.

NextMove Plus

What does Plus include?

Unlimited full reviews, puzzles, Puzzle Rush and drills from every mistake, full-length opening lines, the whole gambit lab, deeper analysis, and Plus backgrounds and badge. Playing is always free.

How do I cancel?

Settings → NextMove Plus → Manage. It takes one click, and you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Email us within 7 days of a charge if something went wrong and we’ll sort it out.

Playing

The bot is too strong or too weak

Pick a bot near your rating. You can also give a bot a handicap (it starts without a knight, rook or queen).

What is the blunder check?

Against bots, NextMove warns you before a move that gives away a piece. Tap the square again to play it anyway, or turn it off in Settings.
