Stockfish 17.1

NextMove runs the Stockfish chess engine in your browser, compiled to WebAssembly by the stockfish.js project. Stockfish is free software released under the GNU General Public License v3. You can get its complete source code here:

  • Stockfish: github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish (tag sf_17.1)
  • stockfish.js (the WebAssembly build used here, npm package stockfish@17.1.0): github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js

The engine files served by this site are unmodified copies of stockfish-17.1-lite-single and stockfish-17.1-asm from that package.
