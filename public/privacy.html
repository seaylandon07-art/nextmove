Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

← Back to NextMove

NextMove is a chess training site. We collect as little as we can, we never sell your data, and there are no ads or advertising trackers.

What you can use without an account

Everything works without signing up. Your games, drills, ratings and settings are saved in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device unless you create an account.

What we store if you create an account

  • Your username and a scrambled version of your password (we use PBKDF2 hashing, so we can't see or recover your actual password).
  • Your saved progress: games, reviews, drills, puzzle and play ratings, and settings, so they sync between your devices.
  • Sign-in sessions: a random token stored on your device, kept on our side only in scrambled form.
  • An email address, only if you choose to add one, used solely to help you get back into your account.

Where it lives

Account data is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts NextMove. Chess analysis (Stockfish) runs on your own device; your moves aren't sent anywhere to be analysed.

Games you import

When you load games by Chess.com or Lichess username, we fetch that player's public games from those sites' public data services and pass them to your browser. We keep a short-lived copy (about five minutes) so repeat requests are fast. Imported games are only saved if you review them.

Online games

When you play a friend online, moves are relayed between the two of you through our server while the game is on. We don't keep a record of the conversation or game on the server afterwards; each player's own copy is saved to their own account or browser.

Visit statistics

We count things like daily visits, sign-ups, games played and reviews run, as plain daily totals. These counts use no cookies, store no IP addresses and can't identify you.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your progress and sign-in token so the site works.

Children

NextMove is for everyone who wants to get better at chess. If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. You can use the site without one.

Deleting your data

Without an account, clear your progress any time from Settings → Reset. With an account, you can ask us to delete it and everything saved with it by emailing hello@nextmove.gg from the address on file, or with your username and recovery code.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on the site. Questions: hello@nextmove.gg.
